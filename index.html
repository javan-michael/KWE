Empowering Our Community

Native Oils is more than wellness; it is an engine for local empowerment. We cultivate our chia seeds right at our village home. By keeping farming, harvesting, and distillation strictly local, we provide sustainable employment and financial independence to the incredible women who nurture these plants from soil to seed.
